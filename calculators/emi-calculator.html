How to Use This EMI Calculator?

An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each month. Our calculator helps you determine your monthly loan payments.

  1. Loan Amount: Slide to select the total loan amount you wish to borrow.
  2. Interest Rate: Enter the annual interest rate offered by your lender.
  3. Loan Tenure: Choose the repayment period in years.

The calculator instantly shows your monthly EMI, total interest payable, and the complete amortization schedule.

Why Use an EMI Calculator?

Financial Planning: Knowing your EMI in advance helps you plan your monthly budget and avoid financial stress.
  • Compare Loans: Compare EMIs from different lenders to choose the best option.
  • Adjust Tenure: See how changing the loan tenure affects your EMI and total interest.
  • Pre-payment Planning: Understand how pre-payments can reduce your interest burden.

Formula Used

The EMI is calculated using the following formula:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1)

Where:
P = Principal Loan Amount
r = Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate / 12)
n = Total Number of Months (Years × 12)
