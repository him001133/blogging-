Understanding SIP Calculator

What is SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a method of investing a fixed amount regularly in mutual funds. It helps in building wealth over the long term through the power of compounding and rupee cost averaging.

Benefits of SIP

SIP offers disciplined investing, power of compounding, rupee cost averaging, flexibility to increase investments, and the ability to start with small amounts. It's ideal for long-term wealth creation.

How it Works

The calculator uses the formula: M = P × ({[1 + i]^n – 1} / i) × (1 + i), where M is the maturity value, P is the monthly investment, i is the periodic rate of interest, and n is the number of payments.

Planning Tips

Start early to maximize compounding benefits, increase your SIP amount annually, stay invested for the long term, and align your SIP with your financial goals for better results.
