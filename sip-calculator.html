How to Use This SIP Calculator?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows you to invest a fixed amount regularly in mutual funds. Our calculator helps you visualize the power of compounding over time.

  1. Monthly Investment: Slide to select how much you can invest every month.
  2. Expected Return Rate: Enter the expected annual return percentage (historically, equity funds give 12-15%).
  3. Time Period: Choose how many years you plan to stay invested.

The calculator instantly shows your total investment, estimated returns, and the final maturity value.

Why Invest via SIP?

Power of Compounding: Starting early allows your money to grow exponentially. Even small amounts invested regularly can build a massive corpus over 20-30 years.
  • Rupee Cost Averaging: You buy more units when markets are low and fewer when markets are high, averaging out your cost.
  • Financial Discipline: Automated deductions ensure you save consistently before spending.
  • Flexibility: You can increase, decrease, or pause your SIP anytime.

Formula Used

The calculation is based on the future value of an annuity due formula:

FV = P × ({[1 + i]^n - 1} / i) × (1 + i)

Where:
FV = Future Value
P = Monthly Investment
i = Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate / 12)
n = Total Number of Months (Years × 12)
